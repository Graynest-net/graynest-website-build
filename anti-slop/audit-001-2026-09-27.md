# antislop audit 001

Date: 2026-09-27
Mode: 2 (audit of finished work)
Scope: copy only, every user-facing sentence. No code changed.
Goal stated by owner: professional software development agency site. Honest, clean, marketing.

Design Read: reading this as a services-agency site for Palestinian SMB owners and startup
founders, in a cinematic editorial voice, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2. No `DESIGN.md`
exists, so the dials are inferred from the built site, not from owner direction.

Note: the `antislop-copywriting` skill is not installed next to the core, so this audit runs on
core rules only (R-02, R-15, R-16, R-17, R-18, R-28, R-36, R-38 plus C-1, C-3, C-5).

---

## What already passes

Worth stating, because these are the usual failure points and the site is clean on all of them:

- **R-17** no statistics anywhere. No "10K+ users", no uptime figures, no invented numbers.
- **R-18** no testimonials section, no invented customers, no stock avatars.
- **R-28** no FAQ, so no generic filler questions.
- **R-16** no marketing buzzwords. Scanned for the full list (AI Powered, seamless, revolutionary,
  cutting edge, next generation, leverage, empower, unlock, robust, world class). Zero hits.
- **R-24** every nav and footer link resolves to a real page or a real mailto/Instagram URL.
- The best copy on the site is genuinely good and specific: "YOUR SHOP CLOSES AT 9. Your agent
  doesn't.", "Answers stock and price questions after closing, and reserves the item for the
  morning.", "Tracks orders, handles returns, and answers the same question for the hundredth
  time, politely." Keep this register. The findings below are mostly where the site drifts off it.

## HIGH (Hard Gate, R-02 / R-36 / R-38)

### 1. Em dash in three sentences (R-02)

- `app/software-engineering/page.tsx:64` "...who need the right thing built — web, mobile,
  backend, and AI where it pays off."
- `components/OfficeScrollFilm.tsx:261` "Flip theme to change the shift — people by day, the
  persona by night."
- `components/agent/AgentDrawer.tsx:215` "Mic is live. Interrupt anytime — the agent will listen
  again."

Fix: replace with a colon, comma, or period. The software hero reads better split into two
sentences.

### 2. A static mock is labelled "LIVE DEMO" (R-38)

`app/ai-agents/page.tsx:199`. The card labelled LIVE DEMO holds two hardcoded chat bubbles and a
hardcoded "typing..." indicator. Nothing is live. The real live agent is the "Ask GrayNest"
drawer, elsewhere on the page.

This is the single most damaging line on the site for an honesty-first agency: it presents a
picture as a working product. R-38 requires real content or an honest placeholder.

Fix: relabel to what it is ("SAMPLE CONVERSATION", "HOW IT SOUNDS"), or wire the card to the real
agent. Drop the fake "typing..." either way.

### 3. Microphone privacy claim is not verifiable as written (R-36)

`components/agent/AgentDrawer.tsx:216` "Uses your browser microphone. Nothing is recorded by
GrayNest beyond the live session."

The voice session runs through ElevenLabs. The sentence may be literally true of GrayNest's own
servers while being misleading about what happens to the audio, because a third party receives and
processes it. A privacy claim stated to a user at the moment they grant mic access has to be
accurate.

Fix: state it plainly, for example "Your audio is processed by our voice provider to run the
conversation. GrayNest does not store recordings." Only claim what is actually true of the setup.

### 4. Privacy policy omits what the site actually does (R-38)

`app/privacy/page.tsx:17` claims "We collect only the information you choose to share with us when
you contact the team or use a GrayNest demo."

Two things contradict this:
- `app/layout.tsx:54` runs Vercel Analytics on every production page view.
- The agent drawer sends microphone audio and chat text to ElevenLabs.

The page mentions no analytics, no third-party processors, and no cookies (grep: zero hits).

Fix: add a short, honest paragraph naming analytics and the voice provider. This is a factual
document, so incompleteness reads as a false claim rather than as brevity.

## MEDIUM (C-1 / C-3 / R-14 / R-15)

### 5. Identical body copy under four different channel cards (C-1, R-14)

`app/ai-agents/page.tsx:135` renders "A consistent answer, on the channel your customers already
use." four times, once under each of Phone calls, WhatsApp, Instagram + Facebook, Website chat.

Four headings promising four different things, all explained with one recycled sentence. This is
the clearest "generated" tell left in the copy.

Fix: one specific line per channel (what the agent actually does on a phone call differs from what
it does in an Instagram DM), or delete the bodies and let the four labels stand alone.

### 6. Identical body copy under four capability cards (C-1, R-14)

`app/software-engineering/page.tsx:88` renders "A focused build shaped around the people who will
use it and the business it needs to move." four times, under Web products, Mobile apps, Backend
systems, Internal tools.

Same problem as 5, and the sentence is also the vaguest on the page.

Fix: one concrete line each, or drop to labels only.

### 7. "Explore" CTAs (R-15)

`app/page.tsx:35` "Explore AI Agents" and `app/page.tsx:50` "Explore Software". R-15 names
"Explore" as a generic default CTA. These are the two weakest CTAs on a site where every other one
is strong ("Start a project", "Book a demo", "Talk to our agent", "Ask GrayNest").

Fix: match the card's promise, for example "See how the agent answers" and "See what we build".

### 8. Site description claims an audience the site does not serve (C-1, R-38)

`app/layout.tsx:10` "AI agents and custom product engineering with AI integration for startups and
enterprises."

Every other page targets Palestinian shop owners, clinics, restaurants, founders and small
companies. "Enterprises" appears nowhere else and is not supported by anything on the site. The
sentence also says AI twice and "custom product engineering with AI integration" is the clunkiest
phrase in the copy. This is the text that shows in search results and link previews.

Fix: rewrite to match the actual positioning, for example "Voice and chat agents, and custom
software, for businesses and startups in Palestine."

### 9. "Always human." reads as a claim the product is human (R-38)

`app/ai-agents/page.tsx` section heading "ALWAYS ON. Always human."

The intended meaning is presumably that a person is always reachable behind the agent, which the
handoff section supports. Standalone, the line states that an AI is human. On a site whose pitch
is honesty, this is the wrong ambiguity to leave in a headline.

Fix: say the real thing, for example "Always on. Always a person behind it."

### 10. The office film section sells the website, not the work (C-3)

`content/office-film.ts` beat 3 is "ONE CAMERA. Two realities." and
`components/OfficeScrollFilm.tsx:261` reads "Scroll to move the camera. Flip theme to change the
shift...".

This copy is about the site's own production technique and its theme toggle. A prospect reading it
learns nothing about their problem or your service. C-3 asks whether a section exists because the
content needs it.

Fix: rewrite the three beats to say something about the work (people by day, agent by night is a
real and sellable idea). Drop the instruction to flip the theme, or reduce it to a quiet caption.

## LOW (consistency, polish, owner confirmation)

### 11. `generator: "v0.app"` still in metadata (C-1)

`app/layout.tsx:11`. Leftover scaffold metadata, published on every page. On a site that says
"Senior engineers who ship", advertising the generator undercuts the claim. Fix: delete the line.

### 12. "typing..." uses three periods (consistency)

`app/ai-agents/page.tsx:210`. Everywhere else the site uses a proper ellipsis ("Connecting…",
"Full name…", "Message GrayNest…"). Fix: use "…", or remove with finding 2.

### 13. "enquiries" vs "inquiries" (consistency)

`app/ai-agents/page.tsx:49` uses "enquiries"; `app/contact/page.tsx` uses "Send inquiry" and
`app/privacy/page.tsx:22` uses "inquiries". Pick one spelling.

### 14. "create new product value" is vague (R-16 adjacent)

`app/software-engineering/page.tsx`, in "We use AI to remove friction, sharpen decisions, and
create new product value." The first two are concrete, the third is filler. Fix: cut it or name a
real outcome.

### 15. Footer headline duplicates a home section (repetition)

`components/Footer.tsx` "YOUR ROADMAP. OUR problem." is the same line as home principle 03, "Your
roadmap, our problem." Strong line, but it lands twice on one scroll. Fix: vary one of them.

### 16. Two closing CTAs share a construction (repetition)

Home closes "READY TO talk?", software closes "READY TO ship?". The AI agents page varies it well
with "HEAR IT yourself." Fix: vary one of the two.

### 17. Owner to confirm two claims are true (R-38)

Not flagged as false, just unverifiable from the repo, and both are load-bearing:
- "We're senior engineers who build both, and we stay until it works." (manifesto) and "Senior
  engineers who ship" (`app/page.tsx:47`).
- "MVP in weeks." (`app/page.tsx:67`) and "in weeks rather than quarters."
  (`app/software-engineering/page.tsx:19`).

If both are true as stated, they stay as is.

## Marketing gap (outside sentence-level scope, noted once)

The site has no proof of any kind: no named client, no case study, no work sample, no team. That is
the honest consequence of R-17 and R-18, and better than fabricating, but it does cap how hard the
site can sell. The highest-value next step is one real case study with a named client and a real
outcome. That is a content decision for the owner, not a copy fix.
