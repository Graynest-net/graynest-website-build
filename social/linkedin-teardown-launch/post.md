The new GrayNest website is live. 🚀

We're a software house: web and mobile products, backends and APIs, and AI only where it earns its place. Senior engineers, an MVP in weeks, and we stay until it works. Built in Palestine, on purpose.

But a website is only half the story. To launch it properly, we're opening something we think is more useful than a brochure:

🔍 A free teardown of your app or website.

Here's how it works:
→ Our CTO opens your product like any customer would
→ He walks through it step by step, recording his screen and voice as he explains what he sees
→ You get a 15-minute video, plus a written summary: three fixes, ranked by impact, with technical tips for making them
→ It lands privately on your WhatsApp within 48 hours

What he looks at: page speed, usability, the questions your customers can't find answers to, anything visible from the outside that puts their data at risk, how dated it looks, and where AI could (or couldn't) save you work.

It's on us. No commitment, no sales pitch. If you want us to build the fixes afterwards, we'll talk. If not, the review is yours to use however you like.

Why free? Because the best way to judge our work is to see it on your own product.

It's for small businesses with a live app or website where the results don't match the effort. (Not for large companies or ideas that haven't been built yet.)
